About

Toby McDonald is a theatrical lighting designer and BFA Theatrical Production and Design student at Ithaca College. He has designed lighting for Round House Theatre's Teen Performance Company and for productions at Montgomery Blair High School, including Romeo and Juliet, Oliver!, and Caesar and Cleopatra. Toby loves collaborating with directors and crews to help each story find its light.
